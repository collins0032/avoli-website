# AVOLI Branding — Chapter (starter, to expand)

## Name rule
- Full: **Akinwale Vincent Omoniyi Legacy Initiative** (first mention on every page, footer, legal)
- Short: **AVOLI** (everything else — nav, buttons, socials). Never "Avoli Foundation" etc.

## Colours
- Emerald `--green #0E6B4A` (trust, growth) • Dark `#0A4E36` • Soft `#E6F2EC`
- Gold `#E8B44A` (opportunity, warmth) • Dark gold `#C9962E`
- Paper `#FFF9F0` background • Ink `#1E2420` text
- Professional but warm: green for credibility, gold for community energy.

## Logo (v0 in assets/logo.svg)
- Emerald circle + gold ring + white "A" + gold arc (people/rising sun).
- Next step: professional redraw, monochrome + reversed versions, favicon set, social avatars.

## Voice
- Credible for corporates, warm for community. Short sentences. Evidence-led ("50 trained, 120 fed").
- CTAs: Donate • Partner With Us • Volunteer • Sponsor a Programme • Join Our Community.
