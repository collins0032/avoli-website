About AVOLI

Who we are, why we exist, and the change we're building — simply put.

Who we are

The Akinwale Vincent Omoniyi Legacy Initiative

AVOLI (short name, used consistently across everything we do) is a Nigerian community-impact non-profit honouring the legacy of Akinwale Vincent Omoniyi — a life of service, hard work and care for others.

We go beyond training: we equip people, link them to opportunities, feed vulnerable families, and build with communities — then show our results openly.

Mission

To impact lives by creating opportunities for young people, equipping people with relevant skills, supporting vulnerable people and advancing community development.

Vision

A Nigeria where every young person can thrive and no vulnerable family is left behind.

The problem & our approach

Why AVOLI exists

Problem

Skills without opportunity

Many young Nigerians finish school without employable, safety-certified or digital skills — and without connections to jobs.

Problem

Vulnerability & hunger

Rising costs leave families choosing between food, fees and rent. One-off handouts don't break the cycle.

Our approach

Train → Link → Support → Build

Train practical skills (incl. HSE). Link to mentors, internships & jobs. Support families via food bank. Build with partners for lasting community assets.
