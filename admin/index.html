How updating works (2 minutes)

1. Edit in a tab, click the generate button.
2. Select-all the output box, copy.
3. Open the matching file in data/, replace everything, save.
4. Upload changed files + new photos in assets/ to your host (cPanel / Netlify / Vercel).

Photos: phone photos are fine — keep each under ~500KB (use your phone's "medium" share size), landscape works best.
Videos: upload to YouTube, paste the link in a News post or Gallery caption.
Forms: launch version opens the visitor's email app (free, no server). Later: create a free Formspree form and paste its URL into js/main.js — ask your developer, 10 minutes.
Analytics: paste your GA ID in Site tab → appears site-wide.
