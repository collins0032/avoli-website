Safeguarding

Because we work with young people and vulnerable people, safety comes first.

Our commitment

AVOLI is committed to the safety, dignity and welfare of every child, young person and vulnerable adult we serve. We have zero tolerance for abuse, exploitation, harassment or neglect — by staff, volunteers, partners or visitors.

Child & vulnerable-person protection: no one under 18 is photographed, interviewed or enrolled without appropriate consent; activities are supervised; data is kept private; concerns are acted on immediately.

Report a concern

If you or someone else is at risk through AVOLI activities:
1. Ensure immediate safety (call emergency services if urgent).
2. Email hello@avoli.org.ng with subject SAFEGUARDING — confidential.
3. Or call +234-800-000-0000 and ask for the Safeguarding Lead.

Reports are treated confidentially and reviewed within 24 hours. You may report anonymously. Retaliation against anyone reporting in good faith is prohibited.
